Common questions

I got a new phone. How do I get my progress back?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and make sure iCloud is on. Install Sushi Siege and open it: your progress comes back from your iCloud backup. The game never replaces newer progress with an older backup.

I bought something and it did not arrive.

Close the game fully and open it again. A purchase that was interrupted is paid out the next time the game starts. If it still has not arrived, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. When a refund goes through, the game removes what that purchase gave.

I left in the middle of a battle.

Your battle is saved. Open the game and tap Resume on the Unfinished battle bar on the home screen to carry on where you left off.

When do daily rewards and the Chef's Challenge reset?

Every day at 00:00 UTC. The game uses the real time, so changing the phone's clock does not move it.

There is no sound.

Check that Sound is on in the Pause menu or on the home screen, and that the volume and music sliders are up. If the sound stopped after a phone call, tap the screen once to wake it.
