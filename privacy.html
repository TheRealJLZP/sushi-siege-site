Sushi Siege

Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026

Sushi Siege is a game for iPhone made by Jason Lim. It does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and no third-party code in the game collects data.

Your progress

Your heroes, gems and progress are saved on your iPhone. If iCloud is turned on, the game also keeps a backup copy in your own iCloud account, so a new phone or a reinstall gets your progress back. That copy is stored by Apple in your account. We cannot see it or reach it.

To stop the backup, turn off iCloud for Sushi Siege in Settings, under your name, then iCloud. Deleting the app removes the save from your phone.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your name, email or payment details. The game keeps a record of each purchase's transaction number on your phone, only so that each purchase is paid out once and a refunded purchase can be taken back.

Network use

The game asks Apple's website for the current time, so daily rewards cannot be moved by changing the phone's clock. Nothing about you is sent with that request, and we receive nothing from it. Apple's own privacy policy covers its servers.

Children

Sushi Siege is rated 13+. Because the game collects no data at all, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
